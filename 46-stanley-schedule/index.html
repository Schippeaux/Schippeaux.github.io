How this list works

The schedule is organised by fixture type, not by room — every item carries a level and room tag, and you can filter by level or status above. Dashed cards are placeholders with a rough planning allowance (AUD, mid-range, not a quote). Add a link, photo or price to an item (open it, then Edit) and it becomes a full product card. Status runs Undecided → Shortlisted → Specified → Quoted → Ordered → Installed. Prices in US$ are shown as listed and are not converted into the AUD total.
