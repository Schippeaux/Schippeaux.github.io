Synth

The MiniFreak is the right synth for this. It is a step sequencer question that is really a generative-system question.

Six-voice true polyphony (pads and chords work), two oscillator engines per voice across wavetable, FM, harmonic and other types, a modulation matrix, built-in effects, and Spice & Dice randomisation aimed at exploratory sound design.

Pads, plucks, bass, stabs, washes and chords all come from one unit. It is hybrid (digital oscillators, analogue-modelled filter), so vintage analogue grit would be a second-synth conversation later, not now.

MiniFreak audio → Portal inputs C/D (line level). USB-MIDI to laptop for parameter control from Live.

Patterns

A fixed-grid step sequencer is deterministic; the brief is generative. The MiniFreak already has a polyphonic sequencer and arpeggiator with modulation lanes, both reachable by Spice & Dice.

The "responds to what is played" layer lives in Live: probability MIDI effects, Max for Live generative devices, and parameters modulated from Sensory Percussion data. Start there; add a dedicated hardware sequencer only against a limitation actually hit.

First experiment

Hi-hat speed → MiniFreak filter cutoff via Expression Control. Snare velocity → reverb send on the synth. One continuous, one per-hit, both from stock devices.
