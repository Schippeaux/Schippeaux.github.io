Concept

A musical environment to play in, respond to, and be responded to by — three live voices played solo, with a generative layer that listens.

The three voices are electronic percussion (Sensory Percussion 2 on a compact mesh-head kit), bass guitar, and the Arturia MiniFreak. Ableton Live is the backbone for recording, looping, routing and the "intelligence" layer, but it is driven from hardware so the computer stays out of the way during performance.

The reference is the Arthur Hnatek / Jojo Mayer (Nerve, Me/Machine) model: a system that reads dynamics, hit patterns and timing and responds. The vocabulary here leans melodic techno — a smaller and faster language to become fluent in, and which the simplified kit suits well.

Principles

Ableton is the brain. Hardware is the hands.

Anything touched mid-performance is a knob, pad or pedal.

Fewer voices, more expression per voice.

A snare with four trained zones beats eight pads with one each.

Commit tone early in the analogue chain.

Keep Live's effects for what is looped, modulated or automated live.

Buy toward a problem that has been hit.

The MiniFreak's own sequencer and arpeggiator come first; a hardware step sequencer only if they run out.
