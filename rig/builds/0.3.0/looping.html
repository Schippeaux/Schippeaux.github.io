Looping

How to loop drums, bass, and synth layers so you can play other instruments and build up a piece.

Method: Session View clip recording

Ableton's Session View is the looper. Fixed-length recording captures a clip of exactly N bars and starts looping it without stopping playback.

Every voice can be looped independently. Audio loops of the drums and bass free your hands for the synth. Audio loops of the MiniFreak free it for new layers — the synth can be re-patched while its last phrase keeps playing. Drums can also be looped as MIDI (the raw zone/velocity data) and re-routed to a different creature or voice.

Fixed-length recording in Live: set a length (1, 2, 4, 8 bars) and press record — the clip ends and loops automatically. No foot-tap to close. This is the core looping mechanism.

How to control loops

Hands are on the instrument. Loop control has to come from somewhere else.

  • Push 1, first: already owned, already has Fixed Length mode. Clunky for looping because it is a reach from the drums, but free and familiar.
  • Compact MIDI foot controller: a small floor pedal that sends MIDI CC or notes. Candidates: Morningstar MC6 (6 switches, programmable), Keith McMillen SoftStep 2 (10 pads, pressure-sensitive), or a simpler 2–4 button unit. Minimal control vocabulary: record/overdub, stop clip, next scene, undo.
  • Trained SP2 zone as a loop trigger: zero footprint — train a Sensory Percussion zone (e.g. a rim tap or shell hit on a specific drum) to send a MIDI note mapped to Live's record action. No extra hardware; the drum is the controller.
  • Small grid controller: Launchpad Mini, APC mini. More visual (LED clip status) but one more thing on the desk.

Note

The Roland Octapad (SPD-30) is a percussion pad instrument, not a loop controller. It plays sounds, not clips. Mentioned here to avoid the confusion.

Example workflow

  1. Set Fixed Length to 4 bars.
  2. Lay down a drum groove. It captures, loops, frees your hands.
  3. Pick up the bass, noodle into a riff. Record a 4-bar bass loop.
  4. Reach for the MiniFreak, play a pad over the top. Record that too.
  5. Now all three are looping. Pick up the sticks again and play live over the bed.
  6. On the turnaround, trigger the next scene — the drum loop evolves, the bass hands off to the synth, the creatures wake up.
