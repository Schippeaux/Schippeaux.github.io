Hook-up

How everything plugs in. The EVANS Portal is at the centre for audio and sensors; a powered USB hub now gathers every MIDI controller before the laptop.

Open questions

  • Portal as sole interface? Bass into input A (inst), MiniFreak into C/D, speakers on Main Out, headphones on phones. If it works, no separate audio interface is needed.
  • Hi-hat pedal: the Portal has 7 sensor inputs (3.5 mm TRS) plus combo inputs A/B, but a hi-hat pedal/controller connection is not documented.
  • USB hub under load: needs testing with everything plugged in at once — Portal, Push 1, loop controller, FX knobs and the MiniFreak's USB-MIDI all sharing one hub and one USB-C run to the laptop. Bus power and enumeration order are both unverified.
  • Push 1 power: Push 1 needs its own power supply — it cannot be bus-powered off the hub alongside everything else.
