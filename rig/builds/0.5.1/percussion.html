Percussion

Sensory Percussion 2 on five mesh-head drums arranged around the seat, plus a kick pedal and a hi-hat pedal at the feet. Expression comes from zones and technique, not from pad count.

What the sensor reads

It does not read acoustic timbre. What the software calls "timbre" is derived from where and how the head is struck — position and strike classification — not from spectral analysis. The raw axes are position/zone, velocity, and speed (hit rate). Everything else in the expression list is computed from those three.

Zone trainingSP2

Each sensor can be trained with up to ten zones per drum: centre, edge, rimshot-centre, rimshot-edge, rim tip, rim shoulder, cross-stick, damped, shell, and a "void" zone for things to ignore. Most players train four to six that they can strike reliably and distinctly; zones can be blended so they flow into each other proportionally rather than switching hard.

Built-in controller types: Timbre, Velocity, Speed and LFO — each mappable to any parameter or sent out as MIDI CC.

The kit

No separate kick drum or hi-hat cymbal. Five mesh-head drums carry all the struck zones — two of them standing in for hi-hat and ride — plus a dedicated kick pedal and hi-hat pedal underfoot. See Arrangement for how they sit around the seat.
