Research

Areas not yet covered that will shape the build.

Latency budget

End-to-end: sensor → Portal → Live → synth → interface → ears. Target under ~10 ms felt; measure each link.

MIDI routing and clock

Who sends clock, who receives, and how Portal, MiniFreak, Push and the controller share one USB host.

Max for Live devices

Expression Control, Envelope Follower, LFO, Shaper, probability tools; which cover tiers 3–4 before writing patches.

Set structure

How a performance is organised in session view: scenes as sections, how a loop is committed or cleared.

Monitoring and room

Low-volume practice: headphone mix, mesh-head noise, neighbours. Whether a small PA is needed.

Capturing sessions

Record every session multitrack by default; how to tag and revisit exploratory playing.

Drumming vocabulary

A melodic-techno drumming language for snare and toms: patterns, fills, dynamics; a learning path independent of jazz.

Bass role in techno

Sub vs mid bass, octave and filter pedals, sharing low end with the synth.

Power and cabling

One power strip, cable runs that do not cross the seat, USB hub reliability.

Visual feedback

What needs to be seen without a screen: a small display, Push's LEDs, or nothing.

Voice routing architecture

Max for Live send/receive bus design for character-to-character signals. How to make routing switchable mid-performance.
