Arrangement

Percussion in the centre, synth and bass either side — a turn of the head, not a move of the chair. The MIDI foot controllers sit between the player and the laptop.

Room plan

Which side bass sits on versus synth is still open — reach and cable runs from the seat will decide it once the gear is in the room. What is fixed is percussion in the middle: it is the busiest voice and the one the seat is built around.

Kit layout

Reusing toms as hi-hat and ride keeps the footprint small: five mesh-head drums instead of a full kit, arranged in the arc a seated player can reach without standing. See Percussion for the zone-training detail and Gear → Phase 5 for sensor counts and cost.
