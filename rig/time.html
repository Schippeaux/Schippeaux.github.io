Time and tempo

Playing without a click and letting Live follow.

Live 11 introduced a built-in Tempo Follower. Before that it was BeatSeeker, a paid Max for Live device from Queen Mary's Centre for Digital Music.

Either listens to a rhythmic audio signal and moves Live's tempo to match. Switching between fixed and following modes can be mapped to a footswitch.

What it tolerates: subtle fluctuation, ordinary push-and-pull. What it struggles with: sustained drift — speeding up or slowing down over several bars — where it can latch onto the initial tempo and let the grid slip away.

This needs testing with the real kit. A hybrid where the drums set tempo at the start of a section and the grid holds for the loop may be the practical answer.

Clock master

If the MiniFreak's sequencer is synced to Live, following tempo moves everything together. If it runs free, loops recorded into Live are unquantised and the feel is looser.
